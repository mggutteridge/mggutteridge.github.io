This page reads Realtime Trains data through a small proxy, because RTT's terms require your API token to stay server-side, not inside a public page's source code. Deploy the included rtt-proxy-worker.js to a free Cloudflare Worker, then set PROXY_BASE near the top of this file's script to your worker's URL. Full steps are in README.md.
Barrier timing is a guesstimate: closed from 2m30s before a train is due at Mortlake until 30s after it clears, using RTT's live & scheduled times. Real barrier behaviour depends on signalling this page can't see — treat it as a heads-up, not a safety system, and always obey the actual barriers and lights at the crossing.
